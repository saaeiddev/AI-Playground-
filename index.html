04 / LEARN THE MECHANICS

From signals to intelligence.

01

Forward propagation

Each layer transforms the previous layer's activations. The final sigmoid produces a probability from 0 to 1.

02

Loss & gradients

Binary cross-entropy measures prediction error. Backpropagation applies the chain rule to calculate each weight's gradient.

03

Optimization

SGD adjusts weights by their gradients; Adam adapts updates using first and second moment estimates.
